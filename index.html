About

I work at the intersection of process automation, software architecture and AI.

I am a process automation manager and practitioner focused on the design and governance of systems that automate work. My interests span workflow and orchestration, low-code engineering, intelligent automation, agentic systems, enterprise platforms and human–machine collaboration.

This site is the index of what I write, research and build. The work itself lives where it belongs: publications on their original platforms, projects in their repositories, and research in dedicated spaces.
